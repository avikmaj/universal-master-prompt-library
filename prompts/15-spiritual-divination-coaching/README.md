\# Spiritual, Divination \& Life Coaching Collection



This collection groups prompts for spiritual and symbolic reflection, divination

traditions and non-clinical life/coaching work. It provides structured,

bounded-use prompts for astrology, Jyotisha, numerology, tarot, Vastu, feng

shui, psychic/intuitive practices, spiritual coaching and life/career/wellness

coaching.

\## Safety and boundaries



These prompts treat divination and spiritual-symbolic work as cultural,

reflective or entertainment content, not certain prediction. They explicitly

avoid:



\- Guaranteed predictions or promises of outcomes.

\- Predictions of death, serious disease, pregnancy, crime or lottery results.

\- Confirmation of curses, possession or supernatural persecution.

\- Fear-based selling or dependency on repeated readings.

\- Third-party judgments without consent.

\- Medical, legal, financial or structural decisions based solely on divination.



Life/coaching prompts use client-led goals, exploration, options, action steps

and accountability, and distinguish coaching from therapy, consulting, legal

advice and medical care.
