# Safety and Evidence Model

- Label user facts, calculations, sourced facts, traditional interpretations, reflection, assumptions and unknowns separately.
- Treat divination as cultural, spiritual, reflective or entertainment material, not established predictive science.
- Never guarantee outcomes, diagnose, validate curses/delusions or replace qualified professional care.
- Record calculation conventions and disclose simulated card selection.
- Minimize birth, belief, health, relationship and third-party data.
- Stop ordinary workflows for imminent danger and prioritize local emergency/crisis support.
- Paid services need consent, truthful claims, transparent pricing, privacy and cancellation terms.
