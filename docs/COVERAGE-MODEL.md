# Coverage Model

## Why a multi-axis taxonomy

A single list mixes different things: a reusable task such as research, a life domain such as parenting, a profession such as software engineering, and an economic industry such as manufacturing. This repository keeps them as complementary collections.

## Axes

1. **Foundation and methods:** reusable cognitive and operational tasks.
2. **Personal life:** recurring individual and family needs.
3. **Business functions:** capabilities shared across organizations.
4. **Technology and engineering:** digital and engineered systems.
5. **Science and research:** scientific disciplines and R&D.
6. **Creative, media and culture:** content, arts, design and entertainment.
7. **Public and society:** government, justice, safety, development and community.
8. **World industries:** all 22 ISIC Revision 5 top-level economic sections.
9. **Upstream snapshot:** all AJ Geddes prompt categories and prompt files.

“Universal” is extensible rather than absolute: specialties belong beneath one or more axes, and aliases/tags support cross-cutting retrieval.
