# Research Basis

The taxonomy separates calculation-heavy, interpretive, spatial, coaching, ethical and historical workflows to avoid silently mixing traditions. Astrology prompts require calculation provenance. Coaching prompts use client-led goals, listening, awareness, action and accountability while distinguishing coaching from therapy and regulated advice.

Key design references include Swiss Ephemeris documentation, Encyclopaedia Britannica entries on astrology/divination/numerology, the Metropolitan Museum of Art's tarot history, ICF competencies and ethics, FTC psychic-service enforcement, and privacy guidance for belief data.
